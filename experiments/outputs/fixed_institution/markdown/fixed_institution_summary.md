# Fixed-institution benchmark summary

## Data

- 30,800 completed games;
- 200 matched seeds per build-mode × population × institution cell;
- seven behavioral populations;
- eleven exchange institutions;
- development-oriented building is the main slice used in the paper.

## Main descriptive result

Central clearing has the highest mean final total score in every behavioral population in the development-oriented slice. The strongest worst-population mean is **Central clearing** at 144.97. The largest across-population score range occurs for **Public pool** at 116.70 points.

## Top institution by population

- **Competitive:** Central clearing (145.06)
- **Cooperative:** Central clearing (145.06)
- **Fairness-sensitive:** Central clearing (146.40)
- **Hoarding:** Central clearing (158.66)
- **Mixed:** Central clearing (147.22)
- **Need-based:** Central clearing (159.32)
- **Selfish:** Central clearing (144.97)

## Public pool versus central clearing

For the cooperative population, the paired public-pool minus central-clearing difference is -4.46 points (95% CI -7.64 to -1.29; n=200). Positive values favor the public pool.

## Build-mode comparison

Across institutions and populations, the unweighted mean crown-aware minus development-oriented score difference is -0.85. The paper treats build mode as a secondary comparison rather than the main behavioral result.

The institutions are bundled protocol packages, so these comparisons do not identify the causal effect of a single institutional dimension.
